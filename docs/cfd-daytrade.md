# CFD and daytrade delivery

The US route now uses the Vantage **US stock/ETF CFD** inventory, not the old 600-name equity watchlist. Forex exposes XAUUSD, XAGUSD and seven FX majors. The complete broker inventory remains in the connection store. US classification requires a broker US folder or exchange; USD denomination and a familiar ticker do not prove a US listing. Unknown regional mappings remain explicitly pending. The new optional path/exchange/underlying metadata is backward compatible with existing connector packets. Underlying volume is **pending**; no tick volume is substituted for exchange volume and no new Webull/FMP subscription is presumed.

## One research rule

`daytrade-trend-15m-v1` uses 60 or more closed 15-minute bars, EMA20/50 trend and slope, and a directional candle closing beyond the preceding candle's high/low. There is one plan per instrument, rather than parallel Breakout/Pullback copies. Thai stocks and DR are long only. TFEX supports both directions; CFD shorts also require the terminal's full trade mode. Disabled/close-only/short-only CFD contracts do not generate this version's plans.

SL is beyond the last eight-bar structure plus 0.25 ATR and at least 1.5 ATR from entry. Plans wider than 4 ATR or with risk below three observed spreads are skipped. Prices use actual terminal point or the existing SET tick table. TP is 2R **before friction**; fees, estimated slippage and observed spread reduce reported R. There is no half exit, runner, or early breakeven. Exit at TP, SL, 16 held bars, or the last day-session bar. US uses the primary New York session. Thai/DR/TFEX use day sessions, exit before lunch, and do not open night-session trades in v1. FX uses the verified broker clock and closes before its day boundary. These parameters are testable starting rules, not evidence of profitability.

## Two distinct histories

- **Replay** enters the next observed bar after the setup. It is calculated over the selected instrument's available stored bars (maximum 3,000), with the displayed cost assumptions. It is not labelled as a live signal or a three-year backtest. A current observed spread is only a historical modelling assumption, not a historical bid/ask record. Unknown spread remains explicitly unknown. FMP intraday volume remains pending.
- **Forward** persists plans, publication times, entry, SL, TP, exit and R in SQLite. It never backfills old bars as published trades. Entry requires a bar that starts **after** actual publication; bars already open when the plan was published cannot become entries. Native data retains the existing 15-minute availability delay. Collection latency therefore changes forward entry timing versus replay. Membership eligibility for signals is unchanged; inactive members can see source coverage/charts but do not receive scan directions or trade statistics.
- Missing tracking bars or a candle touching both SL and TP without a provable opening gap become **REVIEW**, excluded from win rate/PF. SL gaps receive the worse observed open. Drawdown is in equal-risk signal R, not account drawdown. No brokerage orders, position sizing, holdings, or broker P/L are inferred.

Closed observations are retained immutably, and forward IDs are idempotent. Customer CFD scopes use the authenticated owner and exact broker server, so re-pairing preserves that customer's journal without sharing feeds between customers. Revoked/expired connections cannot read the previous private series through the new endpoint. Native licensed Thai/DR/TFEX data uses separate server scopes. All new tables are additive inside the **separate Forex SQLite database**, included by its existing encrypted snapshot backup; membership/D1 migrations and legacy ledger rules are unchanged.

## Always-on collection

The `daytrade` Compose service (scanner profile) collects the existing Thai, DR and TFEX catalogues, one instrument at a time, respecting source rights, complete candles, source delay and API backoff. It keeps running with the browser closed. The existing Swing worker and its ledger are retained; its panel is collapsed and mounts only if explicitly opened. The shared workspace has a one-line market-condition extension to expose the new panel in TFEX; it is not rewritten.

EA **2.30** receives up to eight symbol requests in an authenticated receipt and uploads their closed 15m charts independently of the selected interactive chart. Its file relay preserves these batch requests. Universe resync is six-hourly rather than every 15 minutes. Jobs rotate fairly through the eligible broker catalogue; the API reports every row as queued, warming up, ready or unavailable, and distinguishes ready data from fresh data. Catalogue sync alone never counts as a completed scan.

Historical timestamp protections remain the default. The optional `FOREX_MT5_HISTORY_POLICY=vantage-season-window-v1` permits a maximum seven-day bootstrap after the current clock is independently verified and matches Vantage's [published GMT+2/+3 policy](https://global.vantagehelpcenter.com/hc/es/articles/10955835737871--C%C3%B3mo-cambio-la-hora-que-se-muestra-en-la-plataforma-de-operaciones). It accepts only the portion of the lookback in which both the New York-close and European seasonal interpretations agree with the observed offset. It stops at either transition and disables bootstrap while those seasonal interpretations disagree. This is explicitly a broker-policy inference, not independent historical UTC attestation. Without that opt-in, the original observed-period protection applies. The durable collector accumulates accepted bars across packets, while a new MT5 symbol still remains warmup until actual history arrives. An old EA can still connect but cannot supply the new path metadata or batch collector; it must be reloaded after installation. Actual broker acceptance must verify regional folders, batch uploads, candle timestamps and coverage rather than relying on synthetic tests.

This release does **not** relocate MT5 off the operator's Mac, renew its seven-day bridge credential, or grant Vantage redistribution rights. These are separate remaining operations. Closing the Mac still interrupts its CFD source until the EA is migrated to an always-on host. No customer feed is promoted into a shared operator feed.
